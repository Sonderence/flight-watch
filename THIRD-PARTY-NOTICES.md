# Third-party assets

The locally bundled `web/dist/assets/united.ico` is United Airlines' public website icon, downloaded from [united.com/favicon.ico](https://www.united.com/favicon.ico) on October 29, 2026. It is used solely to identify United Airlines in this portfolio flight tracker. United Airlines' names, trademarks and logo remain the property of their owner. This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by United Airlines.

Other airlines use their IATA/ICAO code as a text badge, not an invented or mismatched logo. An unknown carrier uses a generic aircraft mark. No logo API subscription is required.

The interface requests DM Sans and Manrope through Google Fonts. Font fallbacks are provided when the fonts are unavailable. Google Fonts fonts are distributed under their individual open font licenses; see [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans/license) and [Manrope](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope/license).

Live flight data is supplied by [AeroDataBox](https://aerodatabox.com) and remains subject to its plan, attribution and retention terms. No real provider responses or credentials are included in screenshots or demo fixtures.
